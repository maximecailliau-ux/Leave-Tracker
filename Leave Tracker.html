Public holidays

Fetches a year's public holidays (all states) from a free public holiday database and adds them below, purging anything already in the past to keep this tidy. Worth a spot check against the Fair Work Ombudsman the first time you use this for a given year — general-purpose holiday databases sometimes miss very local quirks (e.g. Ekka Wednesday, AFL Grand Final Friday) that a hand-checked list would catch.
